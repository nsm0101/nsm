CloseDose

For Clinicians & Caregivers

Pediatric tools, built to share

CloseDose is a small collection of free, open pediatric clinical tools built by a practicing physician. Each one is designed to be fast, accurate, and genuinely useful at the bedside — and shared as an outlet for fellow providers and caregivers to use.

The Tools

Dosing

Dosing Calculator

Weight-based acetaminophen, ibuprofen, and antihistamine dosing for infants and children. Choose an age range, enter a weight in lbs or kg, and get clear, dose-capped guidance that is ready to read at the bedside.

Open Calculator
Simulation

PALS Code Simulator

A first-person, voice-driven 3D pediatric resuscitation simulator. Run a code, call your interventions out loud, and rehearse the PALS algorithms in real time — solo or as a team.

Launch Simulator
Boards

ABP Board Prep

A study system for the ABP General Pediatrics certifying exam, weighted to the official content outline. See which domains actually carry the points, work a nine-week plan, review high-yield content by domain, and drill timed practice questions.

Open Board Prep

About

These tools are created and maintained by Nickolas Mancini, MD, MBA, and offered free of charge for educational use. If something here saves you time or helps a patient, that is exactly the point.

They are works in progress and not a substitute for clinical judgment — always confirm dosing and management with your own institution's resources. More about the broader project lives at closedose.com.
